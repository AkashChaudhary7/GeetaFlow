# GeetaFlow — AI Illustration & Visual Character Design Guide
*Version 1.0 • Aesthetic Manifesto, Character Reference Bible, & Prompting Framework*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

**GeetaFlow** bridges timeless spiritual wisdom from the 700 verses of the *Bhagavad Gita* with modern, serene digital mindfulness. 

The visual design language is rooted in **"Modern Indian Minimalism with Hand-Drawn / Warm Doodle Influences"**. 

### The Core Mandate
> **Illustrations must support and elevate the sacred verses — never overpower, distract, or turn into visual clutter.**

The reader's eye must effortlessly rest on the Sanskrit Devanagari calligraphy and its practical reflection. Visual art acts as an atmospheric cushion — inviting warmth, contemplative stillness, and emotional reassurance.

---

## 2. Visual Archetype & Aesthetic DNA

| Attribute | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aesthetic Tone** | "Warm & Expressive", "Cute yet Deeply Respectful", "Gouache & Fine-pen Line-art" |
| **Stylistic Line** | Soft organic contour lines, gentle hand-inked quality, subtle watercolor wash texture |
| **Depth & Shading** | Flat to 2.5D layered planar shading, zero harsh specular highlights or synthetic gradients |
| **Lighting** | Soft golden-hour ambient illumination (*Brahma Muhurta* or peaceful sunset glow) |
| **Emotional Valence** | Reassurance, clarity, deep contemplation, inner quietude, dignity |

### Elements to STRICTLY AVOID (Negative Visual Constraints)
- ❌ **No Photorealism or Hyper-Real CGI**: No glossy 3D renders, uncanny silicone skin, or hyper-rendered pores.
- ❌ **No Excessive High-Fantasy / Sci-Fi Tropes**: No glowing laser eyes, neon energy auras, cybernetic armor, or Marvel-style comic action effects.
- ❌ **No Disrespectful or Caricature Tropes**: No exaggerated comedy facial expressions, clownish eyes, or irreverent cartoon gestures.
- ❌ **No Dark Gothic / Apocalyptic Imagery**: Avoid morbid skulls, pools of blood, fire-and-brimstone doom, or visceral battle gore. Even Kurukshetra is rendered with poetic, reflective stillness.
- ❌ **No High-Frequency Clutter**: Avoid dense background crowds, busy geometric grids, or visual noise that clashes with Devanagari typography.

## 4. Consistent Character Design Reference Bible

To ensure seamless visual continuity across hundreds of daily cards, recurring historical and divine figures must adhere to rigorous canonical reference traits.

---

### A. Shri Krishna (श्रीकृष्ण) — The Supreme Teacher & Inner Friend

```
         /\_/\
        ( ˘⌣˘ )   • Soft, knowing smile (Mandahasya)
       /  |~|  \  • Dusky indigo skin (Shyam-varna: #24416B)
      /   |*|   \ • Pitambari warm gold attire (#F59E0B)
     (____|*|____)• Single peacock feather (Mayur Pankh)
```

* **Physical Form**:
  - Youthful, serene, relaxed posture. Never aggressive, tense, or haughty.
  - Face is round-oval with gentle, compassionate almond eyes and a characteristic serene half-smile (*Mandahasya*).
* **Skin Tone**:
  - Dusky twilight indigo / soft slate blue (`#24416B` to `#1E3A5F`).
  - **NEVER** bright alien cyan, stark smurf blue, or flat grey. It should evoke the color of a water-laden monsoon cloud (*Megha-shyam*).
* **Hair & Adornment**:
  - Soft dark curls tied gently in a topknot tilted slightly to one side.
  - A single, distinctively painted peacock feather (*Mayur Pankh*) with iridescent copper-green and deep gold eye.
  - Minimal, refined gold headband or delicate tiara; never a colossal, ostentatious monarch crown.
* **Attire**:
  - Saffron-gold silk dhoti (*Pitambari*), hand-drawn with flowing minimal drapery lines.
  - Sheer off-white or soft cream sash (*Uttariya*) gracefully draped over one shoulder.
* **Accessories & Props**:
  - Slender bamboo flute (*Bansuri*) tucked neatly at the waist sash or gently held in one hand.
  - Simple natural lotus flower or small string of fragrant white Kadamba flowers.
* **Expression & Demeanor**:
  - The compassionate mentor who listens deeply. Gentle, calm, infinitely patient, reassuring.

---

### B. Arjuna (अर्जुन) — The Earnest Seeker & Noble Disciple

* **Physical Form**:
  - Athletic, dignified warrior-prince silhouette, but depicted in a vulnerable, deeply human, reflective state.
  - Slender, strong shoulders; posture shifts from bowed head and lowered hands in Chapter 1 to upright, centered clarity in Chapter 18.
* **Skin Tone**:
  - Warm golden wheatish brown (`#9C6644` to `#B07D54`).
* **Hair & Headwear**:
  - Dark hair gathered in a functional warrior’s bun or chignon.
  - Understated bronze-gold archer circlet or quiet helmet; often depicted bareheaded when receiving wisdom from Krishna to signify openness and humility.
* **Attire**:
  - Earthy terracotta, muted olive, and deep ochre leather armor plates with soft linen undergarments.
  - Archer bracers (*Anguli-tra*) on wrists.
* **Accessories & Props**:
  - The great bow *Gandiva*, depicted at rest (propped against the chariot wheel or resting unstrung) to symbolize conscious pause over blind action.
* **Expression & Demeanor**:
  - Honest introspection, attentive listening, earnest devotion, transition from anxiety to steady courage.

---

### C. The Chariot & Setting (रथ और कुरुक्षेत्र)

* **The Divine Chariot (*Ratha*)**:
  - Built from warm, weathered teakwood and burnished brass fittings.
  - Four noble white horses (*Sveta-ashva*) standing calm and composed, representing mastery over the four senses.
  - Simple pennant flag fluttering gently with the silhouette of Hanuman (*Kapi-dhvaja*).
* **Environment & Atmosphere**:
  - The battlefield is not depicted with blood or cruelty; it is an allegorical field of human consciousness (*Dharma-kshetra*).
  - Backgrounds feature soft watercolor horizons, rising sunbeams, dust motes glowing in gold light, distant stylized acacia or banyan trees, and calm open sky.

## 5. Master Prompting Formulas for AI Generation

When generating visual assets for GeetaFlow using generative models (e.g. Imagen, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion), use this standardized prompt framework:

### Formula Template:
```text
[Subject & Pose], [Character Specifics from Guide], [Art Style & Medium], [Color Palette & Lighting], [Atmospheric Backdrop], [Composition Rule], --negative_prompt [Negative Constraints]
```

### Example 1: Krishna Instructing Arjuna (Chapter 2, Sankhya Yoga)
> **Prompt**:  
> A peaceful, minimalist storybook illustration of Shri Krishna and warrior Arjuna on an ancient wooden chariot. Krishna has serene dusky twilight-blue skin, gentle compassionate eyes, soft dark curls adorned with a single delicate peacock feather, and is dressed in warm saffron-gold Pitambari silk. He is seated calmly, gesturing with one open hand in quiet wisdom. Arjuna has warm golden-brown skin, seated receptively with hands folded in deep reverence, Gandiva bow resting peacefully at his side. Hand-drawn Gouache illustration style with delicate ink linework, warm Indian minimalist aesthetic. Color palette of Pitambari gold, Yamuna dusk blue, terracotta ochre, and parchment cream. Soft golden sunset light, subtle dust motes in air. Clean negative space at the top for Sanskrit text overlay.  
> **Negative Prompt**:  
> Photorealistic, 3d render, plastic, CGI, western comic style, neon glow, aggressive angry faces, blood, skulls, dark gothic fantasy, cluttered background, distorted fingers, extra limbs, anime exaggerated eyes.

### Example 2: The Steady Mind in Meditation (Chapter 6, Dhyana Yoga)
> **Prompt**:  
> Minimalist hand-drawn illustration of a solitary yogi meditating by a serene lotus pond under an ancient sacred banyan tree. Stylized warm doodle-influence with delicate fluid ink contours and soft watercolor wash. An unwavering oil lamp flame burning steadily in a windless place. Warm amber and deep forest green tones, peaceful twilight atmosphere, stars faintly visible in quiet sky. Cute yet deeply respectful, spiritual mindfulness, spacious clean composition.  
> **Negative Prompt**:  
> Busy, psychedelic, neon lasers, digital artifact, 3d glossy, photorealism, loud colors, clutter.

## 6. Content-First Integration Rules

1. **Card Hierarchy**: In every GeetaFlow card, the typography is the primary hero. The illustration must occupy no more than 35–45% of vertical card real estate, or act as a subtle ambient watermark.
2. **Contrast & Legibility**: Ensure illustration backgrounds near text have a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against the Sanskrit verses and Hindi translations.
3. **Purity of Representation**: Maintain the sacred dignity of the scripture. Characters should always feel approachable yet noble, inspiring reverence and peace.
